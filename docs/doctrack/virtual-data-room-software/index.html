Virtual Data Room Software for Secure Deals

Run fundraising and M&A diligence in a secure, permissioned virtual data room with full audit trails — powered by DocTrack.

A flagship open-source product from the Qik family.

A virtual data room (VDR) is a secure, controlled space to share confidential documents during fundraising, M&A or due diligence. DocTrack's data rooms give every stakeholder the right access — and you the full picture of their activity.

Set granular permissions, watermark sensitive files, track engagement per investor or bidder, and revoke access the instant a deal changes.

Key capabilities

🗂️

Organised rooms

Group documents into structured, easy-to-navigate rooms for each deal.

🔐

Granular permissions

Control who can view, download or print — down to the individual file and recipient.

🕵️

Full audit trail

Every open and download is logged for compliance and deal confidence.

💧

Watermarked viewing

Deter leaks with dynamic, viewer-specific watermarks.

Built for fundraising and M&A

Whether you are a founder sharing a diligence room with investors or a deal team managing competing bidders, DocTrack keeps confidential material secure while showing you exactly who is engaged and serious.

Frequently asked questions

What is a virtual data room?

A virtual data room is a secure online space for sharing confidential documents during deals like fundraising, M&A or due diligence, with controlled access and audit trails.

Does DocTrack include data rooms?

Yes. DocTrack provides secure, permissioned virtual data rooms with analytics, watermarking and full audit logging.
