How electronic signatures work in OpenSign

Upload your document, drag signature and data fields onto the page, add your recipients, and hit send. Signers receive a secure link, complete their fields on any device, and you both receive the finalised, certified copy.

No printers, no scanners, no posting documents back and forth — just a clean digital trail from request to completion.

Frequently asked questions

What is electronic signature software?

It is software that lets you sign documents digitally and collect legally binding signatures from others online, replacing paper-based signing.

Is OpenSign secure?

Yes. OpenSign encrypts documents, logs a full audit trail, and can be self-hosted so sensitive files stay within your environment.
