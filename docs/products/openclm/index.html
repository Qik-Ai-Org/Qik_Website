OpenCLM — Open-Source Contract Lifecycle Management

Draft, negotiate, approve, sign and renew contracts in one open platform. Add AI clause analysis and never miss a renewal again.

A flagship open-source product from the Qik family.

OpenCLM manages contracts from first draft to final renewal in a single, open-source platform. Build from templates, collaborate on redlines, route approvals, capture e-signatures and track every key date automatically.

With a reusable clause library, multi-stage approval workflows and AI-assisted clause analysis, OpenCLM brings order and intelligence to the entire contract lifecycle.

One platform for the whole contract lifecycle

Disconnected tools for drafting, signing and tracking lead to missed renewals and compliance gaps. OpenCLM unifies the lifecycle so every contract follows a consistent, auditable path.

Role-based access, multi-tenant support and a secure API make OpenCLM suitable for legal teams, procurement and fast-growing businesses alike.

Intelligence where it matters

OpenCLM's clause library keeps your preferred language one click away, while AI clause analysis flags deviations and risks during review — so your team negotiates from a position of strength.

Frequently asked questions

What is contract lifecycle management?

Contract lifecycle management (CLM) is the process of managing a contract from creation and negotiation through approval, execution, and renewal. OpenCLM covers all of these stages.

Is OpenCLM open source?

Yes. OpenCLM is an open-source CLM platform you can self-host and extend.

Does OpenCLM support e-signatures?

Yes. OpenCLM includes integrated electronic signature capabilities for executing contracts.
